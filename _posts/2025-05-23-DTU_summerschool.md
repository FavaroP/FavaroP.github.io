I’m excited to share that I participated in the [DTU PES Summer School 2025](https://energy-markets-school.dk/school-2025/)!

This intensive 5-day program, held at the Technical University of Denmark, focused on the latest developments and applications of machine learning and optimization for energy systems. The summer school brought together participants from around the world and featured lectures, hands-on sessions, a poster session where I presented my work on decision-focused learning (see [here](https://orbi.umons.ac.be/handle/20.500.12907/52638)), and engaging social events.
It was a fantastic opportunity to connect with fellow researchers and professionals, learn from leading experts, and deepen my understanding of cutting-edge solutions for the energy sector. I’m looking forward to applying these new insights in my future work!
